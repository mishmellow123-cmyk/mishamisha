# Producers' verdict on DRAFT 1 (cut A, the allegory), 26 Sep 2026

"My main critique of the allegory draft, which I've just watched, is that various parts feel a bit forced.
The fire metaphor isn't particularly clear or conceptually coherent, and much of the rest is pretty on the
nose, so there's an uneven balance between allegory and literal. It also feels a bit rushed and
underwhelming. To land, I think it would have to be a fair bit longer, to be honest. Some of the visuals
are great, though, and there's definitely immense potential. It isn't reached yet, and there's a fair bit
to go, with serious creative thought and taste needed."

Scope decisions: the rethought structure applies to ALL THREE cuts (shared picture and score). Runtime is
NOT prescribed; the creative vision should define it (maybe 3.5-4 min, maybe 6-7). Writers get an OPEN
brief: find your own coherent central metaphor ("fire" is one option, not a requirement).

Standing producer notes from earlier rounds:
- AI labs are neither heroes nor villains: "vehicles of the natural gradient of technocapital, to varying
  extents". The makers' own developers are at most "the closest to a reasonable case for the good guys,
  though as yet undetermined".
- Cringe is a death sentence for this kind of film.
- A = allegory: never says "AI", but the mapping to our moment must be unmistakable. B = wordless legend,
  for its own sake. C = explicitly and lovingly The Lord of the Rings (Misha's cut), not generic fantasy.
- The producers love the ambitious 3-D shots: technically and visually ambitious, but tasteful, never forced.

## Later notes (same evening, after watching cut C too)
- "I reckon the plots should diverge a bit more. At the moment it feels a bit too much like a repackaging
  of the same core thing (from the two I've seen)." So A, B and C should be three DISTINCT FILMS with
  genuinely different plots, sharing the world, look and assets only where that serves them.
- Visual notes: most visuals are great, but a few have slightly shoddy issues or are a bit weird. For
  example, in C at ~55 s the first fire lighting "starts super cool but the way the fire initially
  actually lights seems wrong". The person (heroine) "looks a bit weird", which is not a priority if it's
  costly. (Both are being fixed by the HEROINE department.)

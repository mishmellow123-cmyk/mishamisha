## 1. What we're making

Two short films on one branch, `claude/long-dawn-v2`, in `github.com/mishmellow123-cmyk/mishamisha`. The work dir is `the-long-dawn/`. The grid is 24 fps and 72 BPM; **1 bar = 80 frames**.

- **A · EVERY STEP CLOSER** (~4:30), plus a coded-towers **ALT** master. A real-world allegory.
- **C · THE LAST PAGES** (~4:07 on the new timeline). A Tolkien homage: an illuminated book by a hearth, ink pages that burn through into scenes, an ember/forge world, and ink-drawn mountains. The title card is THE LONG DAWN.
- **B · THE VIGIL** was **DROPPED** by the user ("the story is just so damn unclear"). Its renders are parked; don't revive it.

**Quality bar:** festival grade. Clarity for a FIRST-TIME viewer is paramount: "no one should be straining to make inferences".
- **Caption register:** the user's benchmark is *"They dug deeper every year, for the gold ran deeper still."* It is plain, slightly literary with an old-story cadence, and clear on first hearing.
- **Avoid:** AI-tell words (delve etc.), lines that narrate the picture, lecturing, cringe, and being oblique or abstract.

**The user's standing creative notes:**
- The people movement in the procedural films was "awful and glitchy". Nothing may slide, jitter, pop or walk robotically. Check any motion over 24+ consecutive frames. Prefer still or slow silhouettes and small figures.
- "Unevenly abstracted": some bits are too abstract to follow, one line was too on-the-nose.
- **Transitions must be polished.** The paper burns must look far more realistic. Sound effects must sync to picture.
- **Score:** "great in parts, but in other parts it really just does not work at all".
  - Problems: repeating material after the mood has changed; stretching too long without closure; cartoon-light bits.
  - The user liked the idea of a real sampled orchestra.

## 2. C: THE LAST PAGES (the priority)

### Story decisions, in the user's words
- **The solution is STOP** ("Plan S, not Plan A"). The user later refined it to *"more of a pause-for-as-long-as-it-takes deal"*: keep the film vaguer but directionally the same. Never "destroyed forever".
- **The two front-runners go first, together** (the user's idea, 28 Sep ~08:00Z): the two furthest ahead, hardest to reconcile, light the first two beacons at the same moment; then the rest follow. Keep them abstract, with no national coding.
- **It's a coordination problem.** "Why is it one person... this is a game-theoretic prisoner's dilemma/coordination problem that resists unilateral solutions." So there is **no lone hero**: each smith makes a conditional promise, and the stop happens only when all have joined.
- **Fire:** "trying to cover too much conceptual ground". So each fire now has one job:
  - the gold lettered flame is the Ring's origin;
  - the forges are the race;
  - the plain orange beacons are the promises and the watch;
  - the hearth is the storyteller's fire.
- **Kept lines the user liked:**
  - "Our Ring was kindled from every tale we had ever told." ("Our Ring" OK'd);
  - "It read every word we had ever written, and learned to answer.";
  - "They dug deeper every year, for the gold ran deeper still.";
  - "And the beacons burned on, so no forge could be lit in secret." ("this is great though! verification and transparency!").
- **Rejected lines** (don't reuse):
  - "Ours was being made in every kingdom at once" ("makes no sense");
  - "It woke. No one was behind its eye.";
  - "Ours was forged at every forge at once.";
  - "No one would hold it.";
  - "For once, there was no 'them'.";
  - "The Ring was never finished" (too strong);
  - "But it was ours." (the user queried it);
  - "for it could not be used for good" (queried);
  - "She was no smith" (made her a lone hero);
  - "finished the Ring" (the user didn't love it).
- **The Frodo line.** The user asked whether we can use the film audio of Frodo saying "The last pages are for you".
  - The answer given: it's the user's call. The voice replaces the caption on the blank pages.
  - Build it as a SWAPPABLE stem: a clean version with the caption, and a version with the voice.
  - The clip must come from the user's own copy, must stay OFF the public repo, and is risky for festival clearance and platform content-ID. Keep the score original: no Howard Shore.
